Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information EasyCheck Online collects, why we need it, who can see it, and the choices you have. We keep the language plain so you can make an informed decision about using the service.

Last updated 12 November 2026 Applies to patients, clinic staff and administrators

1 Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to patients, clinic staff and administrators who use EasyCheck Online. It covers the patient portal, the clinic console, and the access-request process.

A clinic may also have its own privacy notice. If a clinic notice and this policy differ, the clinic notice applies to the clinic's own records and this policy continues to apply to the EasyCheck service.

2 Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to run the service. The exact information depends on how you use EasyCheck.

  • Account details from your sign-in provider, such as your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Clinic access-request details, including your name, contact number, the clinic you selected and the status of your request.
  • Clinic and queue information, such as the clinic you follow, live parchi counts, doctor availability and timing.
  • Technical information needed to keep the service secure and reliable, such as error logs, approximate device type and browser type.

3 How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • To show live clinic availability, parchi counts and doctor timing.
  • To review clinic-staff access requests and connect approved staff to the correct clinic.
  • To maintain an audit trail of queue updates and protect against misuse.
  • To respond to support requests, investigate problems and improve the clarity of the service.
  • To meet legal, security and fraud-prevention obligations.

4 Why we are allowed to use it

We rely on the information you provide when you choose to use the service, the agreement between you and EasyCheck, our legitimate interest in operating a safe service, and legal obligations where they apply. Where consent is required, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful use.

5 Who can see information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only when there is a clear operational or legal reason.

  • The clinic you follow or work for, so it can manage its queue and confirm staff access.
  • Trusted service providers who help us host, secure and support the service, under confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, or when necessary to protect the safety and rights of users, clinics or the public.
  • A successor organisation if the service is transferred, subject to equivalent privacy protections.

6 How long we keep information

We keep account and clinic records for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Queue audit records are kept for a limited operational period. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or remove the details that identify you, unless a longer period is required by law or for security investigations.

7 How we protect information

We use encryption in transit, access controls, least-privilege authorisation and audit records to protect the service. Clinic staff can see clinic information only after their membership or ownership is verified. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password for your sign-in account and keep your device protected.

8 Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your information, and to receive a copy of it. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

To make a request, use the contact details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Deleting your account may remove access to the patient portal, but clinic audit records may be retained where the law or security needs require it.

9 Children and young patients

EasyCheck is intended for adults and for young patients using the service with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child without appropriate involvement of a parent, guardian or clinic. If you believe a child has provided information without consent, contact us so we can review it.

10 International processing

The service may use providers in different countries. Where information is transferred across borders, we use appropriate safeguards and require providers to protect it consistently with this policy.

11 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service changes or the law requires it. The updated version will show a new date at the top. If a change materially affects your rights, we will provide a clear notice in the app or by email before it takes effect.

12 Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the EasyCheck support channel shown on the app listing or speak to your clinic administrator. Please include the email address linked to your account. We aim to respond within 30 days.
