Delete your account

You can permanently delete your EasyCheck patient account from inside the app. This page explains what is removed, what may be retained, and how to ask for help if you cannot sign in.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open EasyCheck Online and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings using the bottom navigation.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm the warning.
  4. The app removes your patient profile and signs you out. This cannot be undone.
Open EasyCheck Online Read the Privacy Policy

What is deleted

Your patient profile, including your display name, email address, profile photo link and patient role, is deleted. The sign-in link between this app and your sign-in account is also removed, so the account no longer opens the patient portal through EasyCheck.

What may be retained

Clinic queue and audit records may be retained for a limited operational period where required for clinic safety, fraud prevention, legal obligations or security investigations. These records are not used to sign you in and are not shown in the patient portal after deletion.

If you are clinic staff, your clinic membership is managed separately. Contact your clinic administrator or the EasyCheck support channel to arrange offboarding.

Cannot sign in?

If you cannot access the app, email the EasyCheck support channel from the address linked to your account and ask for account deletion. We may need to verify that you own the account before acting. We aim to respond within 30 days.
